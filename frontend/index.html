🎮 HOW TO PLAY
Rock, paper, scissors… but with pacts, betrayals and a quantum robot!

1🎲 EVERY ROUND HAS A PACT

The game picks one symbol: ✊ ✋ or ✌. It is the pact between you and Q-BOT. You can always see it in the top panel.

2🤔 HOW DO YOU TREAT THE PACT?

With your move, you decide if you keep the pact or not:

🤝 COOPERATEPlay the same symbol as the pact.
🗡️ BETRAYPlay the symbol that beats the pact.
😴 NEUTRALPlay the symbol that loses to the pact. It decides nothing.
💡 Example: if the pact is ✊ → you cooperate with ✊, you betray with ✋ and you stay neutral with ✌.

3⚔️ DUEL!

Q-BOT chooses at the same time as you, without seeing your move. Then a normal rock-paper-scissors duel decides the winner: ✊ beats ✌, ✌ beats ✋ and ✋ beats ✊.

4🏆 TIME FOR POINTS!

🤝 + 🤝 Both cooperate+3 each
🗡️ + 🗡️ Both betray+1 each
💥 Someone betrays and someone wins the duel+5 to the winner
🤷 Any other case0

5🤖 Q-BOT HAS A SECRET PERSONALITY

Its decisions come from a 2-qubit quantum circuit and change depending on how you play. There are three types of rival:

😐 INDIFFERENTDoes its own thing: it does not care what you do.
🪞 RECIPROCALGives you back the same treatment you give.
😤 VENGEFULRarely forgives and gets angry at betrayals.
🕵️ Clue: look at the history. How Q-BOT reacts to your moves shows who it is.

6🏁 15 ROUNDS AND A FINAL BONUS

After 15 rounds, you must guess the personality of Q-BOT. If you are right, you win +15 points! The player with more points wins.

7📷 HANDS OR BUTTONS

Tap ✊ ✋ ✌ on the screen, or press START QUANTUM CAMERA in the player card and play with your hand. Your browser measures your 5 finger angles, and a trained quantum circuit (VQC), not a classical neural network, decides your move. Hold the gesture still for a moment, then remove your hand to play the next round.
